# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 September 2026_

This policy explains how the **Monthly Expenses** app ("the app") handles your information.

## Summary

The app does not collect, send, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## Information stored on your device

- The transactions you enter (title, amount, category, date, and optional note) are saved in a local database on your device.
- App preferences, such as currency or theme, are saved locally on your device.

This information never leaves your device, and the developer has no access to it.

## Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or crash reporting, and it does not connect to the internet.

## Exports and backups

If the app offers export or backup, a file is created only when you ask for one, and it is saved or shared wherever you choose. Once the file leaves the app, the app or service you send it to is responsible for it.

## Deleting your information

Delete individual transactions in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.

## Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
