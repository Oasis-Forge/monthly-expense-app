# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 September 2026_

This policy explains how the **Monthly Expenses** app ("the app") handles your information. It covers the app on Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, and Linux.

## Summary

The app does not collect, send, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device, unless you choose to save a backup or export file.

## Information stored on your device

- Your transactions (amount, category, account, date, and an optional title and note), transfers between accounts, accounts and their opening balances, categories, budgets, and recurring transactions. They are saved in a local database.
- Items you delete, which stay in the app's trash for 30 days so you can restore them, and are then removed for good.
- Up to five automatic backups of your data, saved in the app's private folder before each restore so the restore can be undone.
- Your settings, such as currency, theme, the first day of the month and week, and whether app lock and the backup reminder are on.

The developer has no access to any of it.

## Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting. It does not connect to the internet, and the Android app does not request internet access.

## Backups and CSV exports

- The app creates a backup (a JSON file) or a CSV file only when you ask for one, and you choose where it is saved with your device's own file dialog.
- A backup contains all of your app data and the settings above, except app lock and the backup reminder. A CSV export contains the transactions you are viewing.
- These files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust. Once a file leaves the app, the place you save or send it to is responsible for it.
- To restore, the app reads only the file you pick.
- The app can remind you to back up. The reminder appears only on your device, and you can turn it off.

## App lock

App lock is optional and off by default. When it is on, the app asks your device's operating system to check your fingerprint, face, or screen lock. The app never receives or stores your biometric data or your screen lock code; it only learns whether the check succeeded.

## Permissions

- **Biometrics** (Android `USE_BIOMETRIC`, Face ID on iOS): used only for app lock.
- **Files:** the app has no general access to your files. It reads or writes only the files you choose in the system file dialog.

## Deleting your information

Delete items in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data, including automatic backups. On a computer, uninstalling may leave the app's data folder behind; you can delete it yourself. Backup and export files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

## Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.
